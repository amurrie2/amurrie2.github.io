Project Overview

Application and Purpose

This website will be for AV Construction, a carpentry and home improvement business. The purpose of the website is to promote the business, show previous work, explain available services, and help potential customers contact the business for estimates.

Intended Users

The intended users of this website are homeowners, property managers, and anyone looking for reliable carpentry or home improvement services.

Content Overview

The website will include information about the company, services offered, a gallery of past projects, customer reviews, and a contact page for inquiries and estimates.

Page Design

The website will include the following pages.

Home Page

  • Purpose: Introduce the business and highlight services.
  • Audience: Potential customers visiting for the first time.
  • Content: Banner image, brief introduction, featured services, and quick links.
  • Data Entry: None.
  • Buttons/Links: Navigation links to all pages and call-to-action buttons.
  • Actions: Users can navigate to other pages or contact the business.

About Page

  • Purpose: Provide information about the company and its background.
  • Audience: Visitors who want to learn more about the business.
  • Content: Company history, mission statement, and information about the owner.
  • Data Entry: None.
  • Buttons/Links: Navigation links to other pages.
  • Actions: Users can read about the company and navigate to other pages.

Services Page

  • Purpose: Display all services offered by the business.
  • Audience: Customers looking for specific services.
  • Content: List of services such as carpentry, remodeling, painting, and repairs.
  • Data Entry: None.
  • Buttons/Links: Navigation links and possible contact buttons.
  • Actions: Users can review services and choose to contact the business.

Gallery Page

  • Purpose: Showcase previous projects and completed work.
  • Audience: Potential customers who want to see examples of work.
  • Content: Images of past projects with brief descriptions.
  • Data Entry: None.
  • Buttons/Links: Navigation links and image interaction (click to enlarge).
  • Actions: Users can view and interact with images.

Reviews Page

  • Purpose: Show customer reviews and build trust with new visitors.
  • Audience: Potential customers who want to see feedback from past clients.
  • Content: Customer testimonials, ratings, and short review quotes.
  • Data Entry: None.
  • Buttons/Links: Navigation links and review slider buttons.
  • Actions: Users can read reviews and move through testimonials.

Contact Page

  • Purpose: Allow users to contact the business for estimates or questions.
  • Audience: Customers ready to reach out for services.
  • Content: Contact form, phone number, and email information.
  • Data Entry: Users will enter name, email, phone number, and message.
  • Buttons/Links: Submit button and navigation links.
  • Actions: Users can submit a form to contact the business.

Dynamic Functionality

  • Gallery Interaction: Users can click project images to view a larger version.
  • Contact Form Validation: JavaScript will check that required fields are completed before submission.
  • Homepage Slideshow: The home page may include a rotating banner showing featured work.
  • Review Slider: Customer reviews may rotate or be controlled with buttons.
  • Services Accordion: Service descriptions may expand and collapse when clicked.
