Website Evaluations

Top Rank Boxing

The Top Rank Boxing website is well-designed because it uses strong visuals, clear organization, and consistent styling to present boxing events, fighters, and news in a professional way, especially through its homepage fight banner and structured sections.

  • Contrast: The homepage uses a dark background with bright images of fighters and bold white text, making key events stand out.
  • Repetition: The site consistently uses the same color scheme, fonts, and layout for sections like "Latest News," "Fighters," and "Shop," which creates a uniform look.
  • Alignment: Content is arranged in a grid layout with clean spacing. Sections such as fight cards, news articles, and products are aligned evenly, making the page easy to navigate.
  • Proximity: Related items are grouped together, such as upcoming fights, fighter profiles, and merchandise, helping users quickly understand different sections of the page.

Yale School of Art

The Yale School of Art website is an example of poor design because it is cluttered, inconsistent, and difficult to navigate due to its chaotic layout and overwhelming visuals.

  • Contrast: The site uses too many bright colors and busy backgrounds, which makes the text hard to read and visually overwhelming.
  • Repetition: There is little consistency in fonts, colors, and layout, making the design feel random instead of unified.
  • Alignment: Many elements are not properly aligned, and content appears scattered across the page.
  • Proximity: Information is cluttered together without clear grouping, making it difficult to tell what content belongs together.
